## Overview

FunnelWorkForce is one of my first projects, built for a client who wanted an application similar to Upwork: a marketplace that connects employers with freelancers. It has two sides, each with its own dashboard, profile and "my jobs" views. Employers can browse a directory of freelancers, and freelancers can browse and open job details.

I built the app on my own; my partner handled the design. We deployed it for the client on GoDaddy hosting.

## What’s in it

- **Sign up and login**, with separate flows for employers and freelancers.
- **A seven-step job-posting wizard:** get started, description, details, expertise, visibility, budget, then review and post.
- **Employer side:** dashboard, company profile, freelancer directory, job details and posted jobs.
- **Freelancer side:** dashboard, profile, job details and my jobs.

## Scope

The code in this repository is the application’s front end: PHP page templates built with Semantic UI components and jQuery, covering every screen in both the employer and the freelancer journeys.

## Looking back

This was one of my first client builds, and the gap between it and how I work now is the point of keeping it here. Today I’d start from the data: a marketplace is relational by nature (users, jobs, proposals, contracts, reviews), so I’d model it in PostgreSQL with a typed ORM and role-based auth for employers and freelancers. The screens would be shared, typed components instead of pages with duplicated markup and inline styles, and the seven-step job wizard would be a single validated form that saves drafts. And instead of uploading files to shared hosting, every merge would deploy through a CI pipeline with preview environments.
