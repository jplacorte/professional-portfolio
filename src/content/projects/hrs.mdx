## Overview

HRS (Human Resource System) is an internal tool I built in 2020 at my first company, Startup Project Ventures, to manage our employees. Rather than subscribing to an off-the-shelf employee management platform and paying per seat every month, we built the parts we actually needed and owned them.

It covered two jobs:

- **Employee directory.** Every employee's role, department, status (active, on leave, terminated), hire date and location, in a grid view or a list view.
- **Attendance.** Employees recorded **time in and time out**, giving HR one place for attendance.

## Why build instead of buy

Most HR SaaS tools charge per employee per month and bundle features a small company never uses. Our needs were narrow: records and attendance. Building in-house turned a recurring subscription into hosting costs, kept employee data under our control, and let the tool follow our process instead of the other way round.

## Architecture

A React single-page app talked to a Node.js and Express REST API, which stored employee records and time in / time out entries in MongoDB. The app was deployed on an AWS EC2 instance.

## The 2025 re-upload

The repository linked here is a 2025 re-upload, kept as a milestone. It's set up as a modern `create-t3-turbo` monorepo (Next.js, tRPC, Expo), moves the data layer from MongoDB to PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM, and rebuilds the employee directory as its first screen. It's a snapshot of the idea, not the code that ran in 2020.

## Status

Complete and no longer maintained. It served its purpose at Startup Project Ventures and is kept here as one of my early milestones.
